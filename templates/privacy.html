Privacy

Family Manager runs on your own machine, and your data stays there. It isn't a hosted service and has no users other than your household.

What it reads

The mail senders you configure, and, with the calendar permission you grant, your Google Calendars. It writes only the events you create from it.

Where the data lives

In the data/ folder on the machine that runs it. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. The only outside calls are the ones you set up: your mailbox, your calendar, and the model that reads your mail.
